Tinymovr M5.4 updater

Installs firmware 3.2.1. Open this file in desktop Chrome or Edge on Windows or macOS. Everything is included; no internet, Python, or software packages are needed.

Connect one powered M5.4 using a serial SLCAN USB-CAN adapter (CANable in SLCAN mode, or compatible adapter). Close Studio, Bench, and other apps using the adapter. Keep power and USB connected until the update finishes. This updater does not move or calibrate the motor.
